Gravity doesn't forgive.
Find the orbit anyway.

Orbit Lab is a real Newtonian gravity simulator. Drag to fling a satellite past the star, watch it crash, escape, or - if your aim is true - close the loop into a stable orbit. Live telemetry tells you how close you came.

Enter the lab
☉

Real physics

Inverse-square gravity integrated at 240 Hz. The same math that keeps the ISS up - no faking.

🚀

Live telemetry

Speed, altitude, periapsis, apoapsis and revolutions swept update in real time as your satellite flies.

🎯

Orbit detection

Close a full revolution without kissing the star and the lab confirms your orbit - near-circular ones get extra credit.

🌌

Presets and chaos

Jump straight to circular and elliptical orbits, a slingshot pass, or roll the dice on a chaotic launch.

No sign-up. No tracking. Everything runs in your browser.
